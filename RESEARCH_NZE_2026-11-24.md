## 1. B40 biodiesel

- **Implied gross claim: 2.658 kgCO2e per litre of B100 allocated to blending.** ESDM release 001.Pers/04/SJI/2025, 3 January 2025, is transcribed as 41.46 MtCO2e for 15.6 million kL of biodiesel allocation. The reported suballocations, 7.55 million kL PSO and 8.07 million kL non-PSO, sum to 15.62 million kL and imply 2.654 kg/L. On a nominal 40% volumetric blending basis this is about **1.06 kg per litre of B40**, conditional on the release's method and full allocation. The workbook records 12.11 million kL delivered by 6 November 2025, so the allocation ratio gives no independent evidence of realized emissions savings. See `D_Solusi_Kuantif` rows 24 and 68 and `D_Register_Sumber` rows 475 to 476. [ESDM press-release index](https://www.esdm.go.id/id/media-center/arsip-berita)
- **The subsidy gap per litre remains open.** Obtain monthly ESDM HIP BBN biodiesel and the fossil diesel benchmark actually used for settlement, the eligible B100 deliveries by supplier/month, and the reimbursable transport component. Compute the weighted gap as total applicable payments divided by the eligible litres for the same period. Retain both benchmarks and transport in separate columns. The workbook's Rp35.5 trillion has unclear period and scope, and the 15.6 million kL includes non-PSO allocation. Dividing these gives Rp2,276/L as arithmetic only. It is unsuitable as a paid subsidy rate. See `D_Register_Sumber` row 478 and Kepmen 341.K/EK.01/MEM.E/2024 for the allocation lead. [ESDM legal database](https://jdih.esdm.go.id/) [BPDP](https://www.bpdp.or.id/)
- **Fossil combustion cross-check (AC/A).** IPCC defaults of 74.1 gCO2/MJ and 43.0 MJ/kg for gas/diesel oil, with an explicitly assumed density of 0.84 kg/L, imply about **2.68 kg fossil CO2 per litre of B0**. Displacing 0.4 L of B0 per litre of B40 gives **1.07 kg fossil CO2/L of blend** under a simple equal-volume comparison. This illustrates the scale of the ESDM ratio. It omits differences in delivered energy, upstream emissions, methane, and land-use change. [IPCC 2006 Guidelines, Vol. 2 Ch. 1 Table 1.2](https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2006gl/pdf/2_Volume2/V2_1_Ch1_Introduction.pdf) [Vol. 2 Ch. 3 Table 3.2.1](https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2006gl/pdf/2_Volume2/V2_3_Ch3_Mobile_Combustion.pdf)
