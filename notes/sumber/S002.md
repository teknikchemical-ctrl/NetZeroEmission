## Kesimpulan (ekstrak otomatis)
This study provides the first spatially explicit, national-scale assess­ ment of the climate change mitigation potential of producing wood products from restored degraded lands in Indonesia by integrating a GISbased land suitability analysis with a dynamic material flow and life cycle assessment (MFA–LCA) framework. Across the developed degraded land scenarios (DLS1–DLS3), between 0.44 and 3.60 Mha of land were found suitable for selected plantation species, illustrating the high sensitivity of national land restoration estimates to degraded land definitions and input datasets.
