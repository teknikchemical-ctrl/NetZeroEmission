## Abstrak (ekstrak otomatis)
—This research aims to analyze challenges and solution to achieve NZE (net zero emission) in the electricity sector in Indonesia. Many challenges needed to overcome to achieve NZE target by 2060. The research method used is a literature study, discussion with PLN (state-owned electricity company) stakeholder in the renewable energy sector. Data analysis is done qualitatively using a thematic analysis approach. The research findings explain the obstacles to the development of renewable energy and alternative solutions that can be pursued. The challenges faced are not only technical aspects of renewable energy but also non-technical aspects such as financing, regulation, and electricity market
