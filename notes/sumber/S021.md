## Abstrak (ekstrak otomatis)
KEYWORDS Indonesia, like many countries around the world, has embedded the low-carbon para­ digm into its development plan by reducing energy and carbon intensity. To design the appropriate policy, the government needs information on which sectors have the high­ est energy intensity and what are the main drivers of energy emissions. We investigate the lifecycle energy and emission intensity of industrial sectors by using the Hybrid Energy Input-Output (HEIO) table and Structural Decomposition Analysis (SDA) techni­ que. Applying this to the Indonesian economy from 2010 to 2016, we find that non­ metallic minerals and metal products have the highest total energy and emissions intensity. Up

## Kesimpulan (ekstrak otomatis)
and policy implications We have investigated the energy and intensity of industrial sectors using the case of Indonesia. We analyzed 83 energy and non-energy sectors in Indonesia and estimated their energy and emission intensities using the HEIO Table. Further, we conducted the SDA analysis to investigate the main drivers of energy and emission changes between 2010 and 2016. The study adds value to the IO analysis of Indonesia’s economy by enabling the attribution of energy consumption and emissions to energy sources in their primary and secondary forms.
