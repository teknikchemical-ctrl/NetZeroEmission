## Abstrak (ekstrak otomatis)
Indonesia’s power sector is still dominated by fossil fuels, which makes it difficult for the country to meet its climate goals. This study develops an integrated modeling framework to evaluate long term decarbonization pathways that combine a carbon tax (CT), an emissions trading system (ETS), and carbon capture and storage (CCS). We use a generation expansion model based on mixed integer linear programming and simulate six policy scenarios from 2025 to 2050 to assess impacts on CO2 emissions, generation mix, capital investment, and system costs. ## Kesimpulan (ekstrak otomatis)
MISCAP, an integrated modeling framework, is developed to evaluate long term decarbonization strategies for Indonesia’s power sector. The model embeds CT, ETS, and CCS within a unified GEP formulation based on mixed integer linear programming. Simulating six policy scenarios from 2025 to 2050, MISCAP captures interactions among emissions, investment, technology deployment, and capacity growth aligned with Indonesia’s NZE target.
