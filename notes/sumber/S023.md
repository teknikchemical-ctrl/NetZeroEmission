## Kesimpulan (ekstrak otomatis)
Decommissioning CFPP with higher CO2 emissions (particularly prioritising the oldest units) presents a cost-effective strategy; Banten Suralaya, Paiton-2, Paiton-1, and PLN Paiton power plants stand out as primary candidates for early retirement. By retiring these four facilities, CO2 emissions could be reduced by 57.19 million tonnes a-1 annually, constituting approximately 19 % of all emissions generated by power plants in Indonesia.
