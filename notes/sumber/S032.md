## Abstrak (ekstrak otomatis)
: This article uses the Easton theory approach to Analysis of Easton's Systems Flow Model in the Process of Net Zero Emissions (NZE) Ratification in Indonesia. The primary focus of this study is on the input, output, and feedback sections in the political system that support the NZE policy. The method used is qualitative with descriptive analysis to explain each section of the political system flow model. Data collection techniques are done through literature reviews, relevant articles, and news on handling climate change in Indonesia.
