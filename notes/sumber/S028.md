## Abstrak (ekstrak otomatis)
Indonesia’s power sector is still dominated by fossil fuels, which makes it difficult for the country to meet its climate goals. This study develops an integrated modeling framework to evaluate long term decarbonization pathways that combine a carbon tax (CT), an emissions trading system (ETS), and carbon capture and storage (CCS). We use a generation expansion model based on mixed integer linear programming and simulate six policy scenarios from 2025 to 2050 to assess impacts on CO2 emissions, generation mix, capital investment, and system costs.
