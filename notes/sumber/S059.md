## Abstrak (ekstrak otomatis)
Indonesia has committed to achieving Net Zero Emission (NZE) by 2060 at the latest. Behind this discourse, the reality is that Indonesia remains heavily dependent on fossil fuels, which account for 80% of its energy mix, sparking significant public debate. This situation positions the mass media to shape public perception. Various national mass media outlets will frame the NZE 2060 issue with narratives concentrating on either economic interests or environmental sustainability.
