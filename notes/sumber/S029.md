3.7. Policy implication Based on the energy modeling results, several key conclusions can be drawn regarding the pathway for achieving the NZE target in Indonesia. Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage are the most effective mea­ sures, contributing to a 33 % reduction in GHG emissions by capitalizing on Indonesia’s significant carbon storage potential.
