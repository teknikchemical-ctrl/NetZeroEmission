## Kesimpulan (ekstrak otomatis)
Based on the current rate of utilization of biomass waste, less than a third of all residues generated are available for use in biomass co-ﬁring. Diversion of waste products for other purposes does not occur at a ﬂat rate across feedstocks: large percentages of rubber, MSW, and rice husk remain unutilized, while only small fractions of other residues such as wood, PKS, and EFB remain available as feedstock. This likely presents a challenge to the implementation of co-ﬁring since wood and PKS biomass residues are both energy dense, with among the highest caloriﬁc values of all feedstocks - abo
